Agreement by variable

One row per variable at the chosen resolution. Within-site r is the correlation at each site over its paired time steps: the bar spans the 10th percentile to the median across sites, the tick is the median. NSE is the Nash-Sutcliffe efficiency of the shuttle with FLUXNET2015 as reference, 1 for identical series. Across-site r compares the site means of the two products. QC values are near constant, so for QC rows the share of equal values says more than r. QC is compared only where its flux exists in both products.

Site means

Mean of each site over its paired time steps, shuttle against FLUXNET2015, with the 1:1 line. Sites whose means differ by more than 20% are marked.

Per site

Ref only and shuttle only count time steps where just one product has a value, which shows record differences outside the paired steps.

Precipitation without a rain gauge

Sites whose shuttle record has no measured P, so P_F is the ERA5 series of the zip without site correction. Where a gauge exists, ERA5 P matches it. FLUXNET2015 and shuttle columns are mm per year over the common years; blank where FLUXNET2015 has no P in those years.

Hourly time offsets

Hourly variables whose correlation is highest with the shuttle shifted by whole hours. A positive lag means the shuttle series runs later than FLUXNET2015. All other sites and variables match best at zero.

No common years

Sites on both lists whose shuttle years do not overlap the FLUXNET2015 record.
